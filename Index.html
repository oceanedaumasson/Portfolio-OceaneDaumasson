I’m Océane, a Computer Science and Computation Arts student at Concordia University in Montreal.

I spend two years in a Fine Arts degree before discovering a passion for programming and how limitless it feels as a medium for creativity, giving me an alternative perspective to the development process. I’ve experimented with interactive media, game development, generative systems and graphic design. My design process is driven by curiosity. I come up with lots of ideas, try new mediums and tools, learn whatever I need to make them work, and see what happens.

Lately, I’ve been especially interested in how color, mood, and everyday experiences can be translated into meaningful digital works.
