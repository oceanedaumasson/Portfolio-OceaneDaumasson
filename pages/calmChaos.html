CALM VS CHAOS

GITHUB

Max/MSP

A two-part generative audio-visual project built in Max/MSP, exploring emotional contrast through real-time systems. The project investigates how sound, motion, and form can shape mood and perception across two contrasting environments.

Key Features

  • Real-time generative visual system
  • Particle dynamics and chaotic attractor
  • Audio-reactive parameter mapping
  • Timed randomization

Design Process

  1. Ideation

    Started with one question: how can two environments portray two different emotions?

    Chose calm and chaos, then questioned how they might differ visually in shape, color and movement.

  2. Building

    Worked on both simultaneously.

    With every edit to one, I thought about how the other could best conflict with it.

  3. Refinement

    Kept working until the two felt as opposite as possible.

    Incorporated audio to deepen these differences.
